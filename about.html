About

Research Assistant & PhD Candidate, Atılım University.

I am a research assistant at Atılım University. I work on efficient algorithms for wireless communication systems, in particular faster-than-Nyquist (FTN) signaling and demodulation techniques.

This website is a central hub for the tools, schedulers and resources I share with my students and colleagues.
